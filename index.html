How it works

Three rules. That’s the whole game.

01

Swap neighbours to line up three of a kind. Chains multiply the score.

02

Four in a line forge a current, which sweeps its whole row or column. An L or a T forges a burst.

03

Five forge a pearl. Swap it with any creature and it claims every creature of that kind on the board.

The descent

A thousand levels. Five waters.

The levels descend from sunlit water into the trench, through five zones that each read like a different sea. New creatures join the deal on the way down — eight by the end — and in the deep the board stops being a square.

Level design

Every level was proved winnable before it shipped.

Each level is a dealt board with its own goal — reach a score, gather a creature, forge a mark — and each was played to the end by a bot before release: no perks, no favours, inside the move budget, all one thousand of them.

Difficulty is measured, not guessed. The bot’s win rate is the honest number, and the curve is tuned so the difficulty climbs rather than jumps.

And none of them needs anything from the pouch. Bare hands reach the trench floor.

The same thousand levels ship on every device. No server, no daily shuffle, nothing generated behind your back — tide nº 90001 is the same deal for you as for everyone else. It’s the one playing at the top of this page.

Feel

Designed around the swap.

  • A tick under your finger for every match, and a firmer pulse when something forges. Both switchable.
  • Every sound is synthesised as you play — thirteen of them, from the smallest pop to the pearl storm, and not a single audio file.
  • A dead board reshuffles itself, free and automatically. No move is never the game’s answer.
  • Reduce Motion is honoured everywhere — the sea calms down, the game doesn’t.
  • Never colour alone: every creature has its own shape, so two of them never have to be told apart by hue.
  • AA contrast throughout, from the level map down to the smallest caption.

Free to download

The pouch is optional.

Helmi is free to download, and all thousand levels are there from the first launch. What’s for sale is refills of the pouch — more moves, a pearl sweep, a fresh board — and the pouch fills itself anyway: one of each, free, every ten levels down.

No level needs anything from it. No subscription, no ads, and no upsell in the middle of a move.

Every device

Your descent follows you.

Solved levels, free-tide bests and the pouch sync through your own iCloud, so the iPad picks up where the iPhone left off.

There is no account and no server of ours — the record lives in your iCloud, readable by your devices and nobody else.
